Know What Happens Next

Your Build, Step By Step

கட்டுமான படிநிலைகள்

A typical 1,500 sq.ft independent house takes about 8–10 months. Here is how the time is spent.

Week 1–2

Free consultation & site visit

We visit your plot, check soil, road access, setbacks and your budget, then give a written estimate — at no cost.

Week 3–6

Design & approvals

Floor plans, 3D elevation and structural drawings. We handle DTCP / local body approval paperwork for you.

Month 2–3

Foundation & plinth

Excavation, footing, column starters and plinth beam, with cube tests recorded at every pour.

Month 3–6

Structure & walls

Columns, roof slabs and brickwork floor by floor. You receive weekly site photos and a stage-wise bill.

Month 6–8

Finishing

Plastering, flooring, wiring, plumbing, doors, windows, painting and the front elevation work.

Month 8–10

Handover

Deep cleaning, snag list correction, EB connection assistance and handover with all warranty documents.
